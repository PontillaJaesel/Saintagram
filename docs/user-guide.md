# Saintagram User Guide

## What Saintagram is

Saintagram is a private place to build a profile, write reflections, and look
back on your spiritual journey. It is designed for honest sharing, not for
popularity. There are no follower counts, public likes, or popularity scores.
FiAt has an optional activity leaderboard for friendly encouragement, but it
does not measure your worth or the quality of your faith.

This guide explains the usual journey from your first visit to using your
profile. The words in **bold** are names you may see on buttons or pages.

## Your journey at a glance

1. Open the private invitation link.
2. Enter the invitation code.
3. Sign in with your issued username and temporary password.
4. Create your profile.
5. Set a permanent password.
6. Read and accept the privacy notice.
7. Read the spiritual introduction.
8. Finish your profile and begin using Saintagram.

After setup, the main navigation takes you to **Profile**, **Community**,
**Reflect**, **Journey**, and **Settings**.

## 1. Open Saintagram

Use the private link or invitation you were given. Saintagram may first ask
for an **invitation code**. Type the code carefully and choose **Continue**.

The invitation code lets you enter the application, but it is not your
personal login. Keep it private and do not post it publicly.

If the code does not work, check for extra spaces and try again. If it still
does not work, ask the person or organization that gave you the invitation.

## 2. Sign in

On the sign-in page:

1. Enter your issued **Username**.
2. Enter your **Temporary password**.
3. Choose **Log in**.

Saintagram uses usernames issued to you. There is no public sign-up with a
personal email address and no Google sign-in in the normal process.

If you forget your password, use the password-help option if it is available
or contact your support person. Do not share your password with anyone.

## 3. Create your profile

If this is your first visit, Saintagram opens the profile builder. Move
through the sections using **Next**, **Back**, and **Save draft**.

You may be asked about items such as:

- Your name and profile image or symbol
- Your spiritual bio
- People or ideas that guide you
- Your hopes, directions, and things you like
- Posts God Sees
- A Hidden Story

Write only what feels right for you. A draft saves your progress so you can
return later. Before finishing, use the review screen to check your answers.
Choose **Edit** beside a section if something needs changing, then choose the
final save option when your profile is ready.

### About Hidden Story

**Hidden Story** is extra private content. It is kept separate from the
standard profile and requires an additional privacy check before it can be
shown. Even so, choose carefully what you write.

## 4. Choose a permanent password

After the first profile setup, Saintagram may open **Settings** and ask you to
replace the temporary password.

Complete these fields:

- **Current temporary password**
- **New password**
- **Confirm new password**

Choose **Change password** or **Set password**. Your new password should be
private and difficult for other people to guess. Do not use your name, school,
team, birthday, or an easy pattern.

You must complete this step before using the rest of the application.

## 5. Read the privacy notice

The **Privacy and consent** page explains how your information is handled.
Read it with care. Saintagram may contain sensitive information about faith,
identity, relationships, struggles, hopes, or private experiences.

When you understand the notice and agree to continue, select:

**I understand and accept this privacy and consent notice.**

Then choose **Accept and continue**.

You can revisit privacy choices later in **Settings**.

## 6. Read the spiritual introduction

The introduction is a short welcome before your profile. Read the message,
then choose **Create my profile before God** to continue.

This completes the welcome part of setup and takes you to your profile or back
to any remaining profile step.

## 7. Use your profile

When setup is complete, **Profile** is your home base. From there you can:

- View your profile and the parts you chose to share
- Choose **Edit profile** to update it
- Open **Reflect** to write a reflection
- Open **Journey** to see your timeline
- Open **Community** to explore available people,  posts, and special announcements
- Open **Settings** to manage your account and privacy

Your profile is about your story. You do not need to make it perfect before
you begin using the rest of Saintagram.

## 8. Write a reflection

Choose **Reflect** in the main navigation.

1. Enter a **Moment title**.
2. Write in **Your reflection**.
3. Add media or other optional details if you want to.
4. Select **Keep this reflection private** when it should stay just for you.
5. Choose **Save reflection**.

You can return to a saved reflection to edit or delete it. Private reflections
belong to you and are protected as owner-only content.

## 9. Use FiAt to notice meaningful actions

FiAt is a simple way to mark a reflection as a meaningful action or choice.
It is optional. You do not need to use FiAt for every reflection, and there is
no prize for writing the most or moving the fastest.

### Add FiAt to a reflection

While writing or editing a reflection, find the FiAt choice and select the
category that best fits your moment:

- **Prayer**
- **Forgiveness**
- **Service**
- **Sacrifice**
- **Act of Love**
- **Responsible Choice**
- **Other**: add a short description when none of the categories fit

Then save the reflection. You can change or remove the FiAt category later.
FiAt uses the day the reflection was created, so editing it later does not
move the action to a new day.

### Build a streak

Open the FiAt statistics from the FiAt badge on your **Profile**. A streak is
the number of consecutive calendar days with at least one eligible FiAt
reflection. Several FiAt reflections on the same day count as one streak day,
so one meaningful action is enough.

The FiAt area can show your current streak, longest streak, total FiAt entries,
total FiAt days, and recent weekly activity. Your current streak still counts
as active when your latest FiAt was today or yesterday. If a full day is
missed, the current streak starts over, but your longest streak remains saved.

Use the streak as a gentle prompt to notice one meaningful choice each day,
not as pressure. A new streak is still a fresh beginning.

### See the leaderboard

Choose the trophy button in the FiAt controls to open the leaderboard. The
full leaderboard can show **Weekly** or **Monthly** results. It counts eligible
FiAt entries in that period, and people with more entries appear higher. The
leaderboard may show the top five first and can also show your own rank.

The leaderboard does not show anyone's reflection words. Reflections marked
**Keep this reflection private** are not included in leaderboard counts. Use
the leaderboard as an optional way to celebrate steady participation and
encourage others, never as a reason to compare your personal story with theirs.

## 10. Look back at your journey

Choose **Journey** to see a timeline of meaningful activity, such as creating
or updating your profile and writing reflections. Use it as a quiet place to
notice how your story is developing over time.

## 11. Explore Community

Choose **Community** to see the community area. Depending on what is enabled
for your account, you may find:

- **Following**: people or posts you already follow
- **Discover**: available profiles and posts to explore
- **Search people**: a way to look for someone by name
- **Bulletin**: a space for special announcements

Move at your own pace. You never need to share more than you are comfortable
sharing.

## 12. Manage your account in Settings

Open **Settings** whenever you need to:

- Change your password
- Review privacy preferences
- Request a private personal-data export
- Begin permanent account deletion
- Sign out

Account deletion is permanent. Read the confirmation carefully before you
continue.

## Privacy and safety reminders

- Do not enter passwords, payment information, government ID numbers, or
  anything that could put you or another person in danger.
- Keep your username, password, and invitation code private.
- A private reflection is intended for you, but still use good judgment about
  sensitive details.
- If you are a young person, ask a trusted adult for help if a screen or
  question feels confusing, uncomfortable, or unsafe.
- If something feels wrong, stop and speak with your trusted adult or the
  person who gave you access to Saintagram.
- Always sign out when using a shared device.

## Quick help

**I cannot get past the invitation screen.** Check the invitation code and
contact the person who issued it.

**I cannot sign in.** Check your username and password. If your temporary
password has already been replaced, use your permanent password instead.

**I was sent to Settings.** You still need to replace your temporary password.

**I was sent to Privacy or Introduction.** That part of setup is required
before your profile can open.

**I lost my work.** Look for **Restore draft** in the profile builder. Draft
restoration depends on the same browser and account being available.

**I need to leave.** Choose **Sign out** in Settings. On a shared device,
close the browser after signing out.
